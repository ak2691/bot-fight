Machiner Match Components
=========================

Match format
------------
- A match is a best-of-three duel between two player-built bots.
- Each round starts with an ability draft. Players choose from the abilities offered for that round and allocate the stat points available so far.
- Drafted abilities and stat allocations carry forward into later rounds, with new choices added before each round.
- The arena contains the two fighters and entities created by their abilities. There are no placeable arena objects, walls, cores, or pickups.

Training and brain submission
-----------------------------
- After both players lock their round loadouts, each player receives a timed training session.
- During training, players build and test a structured deterministic logic brain in the browser arena.
- A brain is made from prioritized logic blocks and conditions that choose movement, facing, and drafted-ability actions.
- Brains can target the opponent and supported entities created by abilities; arbitrary code and expression text are not executed.
- At the training deadline, the normalized brain and loadout are submitted for the round.

Authoritative fight
-------------------
- The server runs the rated duel using the submitted brains, the deterministic match seed, and the same fixed-step gameplay contract mirrored by browser training.
- Replay frames contain fighter state and transient ability-created entities needed to present and audit the fight.
- Browser simulation is a preview; the server result is authoritative.

Win condition
-------------
- A round is won only by reducing the opposing fighter's HP to zero through damage.
- If neither fighter is defeated before the round time limit, the round is a draw.
- The first player to win two rounds wins the match. If all three rounds finish without either player reaching two wins, the match result follows the recorded round results and tie handling.
- Disconnect forfeits and surrender can also end the match outside normal combat resolution.
