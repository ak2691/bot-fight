Chain stack blocks: reference

docs/CODE_WORKSPACE_REDESIGN.md section 5. No separate header container: an If's conditions are plain rows inside the C-block header, the first starting with a yellow IF and the rest with a yellow AND. The mouth has a solid left edge from the header down to the foot. Every block keeps its own 300 px width, so nested blocks stick out to the right of their parent. The canvas scrolls horizontally once content comes within 300 px of the right edge.
